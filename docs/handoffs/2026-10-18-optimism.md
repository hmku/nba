# Handoff 2026-10-18 - optimism decomposition of the pre-draft projection (quant researcher, cloud session)

## What was accomplished
- New experiment harness `src/fantasy/sim/symmetric.py` + CLI `fantasy sim symmetric`: plays the same draft
  seeds (common random numbers) in rooms where 0 / 3 / 6 / 11 / all opponents draft with our optimizer
  (`adp_all`, `B`, `C3`, `C6`, `C11`, `A_sym`, `A_bpa`, `A_full`, plus `C3m`/`C11m` with market-informed
  opponents), grades every league with the season simulator under three truths, and summarizes slot-1
  results with paired SEs and the symmetric slot curve. Resumable JSON-lines output, multiprocessing.
- `src/fantasy/sim/market_truth.py`: market-implied projection set (quantile map of total value onto ADP
  order via one per-player volume multiplier; Spearman vs ADP 0.78 → 0.999 in the top 156) and a 50/50 blend.
- Ran it: 100 draft seeds x 500 season sims x 3 truths (60 seeds for C3m/C11m, 30 for a half-view-noise
  sensitivity, 9 for A_full). Write-up with tables: `docs/research/optimism_decomposition.md`.
- Headline (slot 1, P(playoffs)/P(title)): status quo 0.965/0.548 (= dashboard); symmetric room
  0.612/0.129; 11 optimizer opponents 0.752/0.251; status quo graded by market truth 0.783/0.197, by blend
  0.906/0.372; sharp market-informed opponents + market truth 0.672/0.120. Central ≈ 83–89% / 28–35%.

## Decisions & rationale
- Opponents' "our strategy" = optimizer restricted to the balanced strategy: on 9 seeds the full-scan
  optimizer for all 12 teams produced identical picks (1,404/1,404), at 1/7 of the cost.
- Smart opponents need private views (else 12 identical optimizers give one deterministic draft): our
  projections + the season simulator's projection-error prior (sds 0.08/0.08/0.015/0.03). This is a prior;
  the information edge (C11 − A_sym) is sensitive to it (title +0.123 at prior, +0.055 at half).
- Market truth moves only per-game volume (keeps category mix/percentages/gp): simplest construction that
  matches ADP order; limits documented.
- Did not edit web/, engine.py, lines.py or the value-search files.
